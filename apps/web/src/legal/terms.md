already know. **Wortgarten currently teaches German only.**

## AI-generated content — please read this

**Stories and their cover images are machine-generated.** They can contain mistakes: awkward
German, inaccurate translations, or content that doesn't quite make sense. Wortgarten's code always
chooses which vocabulary a story practices, but the AI model writes the actual text, and small
models make mistakes.

**Treat generated stories and images as practice material, not a source of truth.** Do not rely on
them for exams, professional translation, or any legal, medical, or otherwise high-stakes context.
