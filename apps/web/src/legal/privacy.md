# Privacy Policy

**Status: draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Do not rely on this page as final.**

This page explains what Wortgarten collects, why, and who it's shared with. It's written in plain
language on purpose — if anything here is unclear, please tell us.

## What we collect

**Account**
Your name, email address, and a password hash (if you sign up with email) or your Google profile
(name, email, avatar) if you sign in with Google. We also store login session records — including
IP address and browser/device info — for each time you sign in, so we can keep you logged in
securely.

**Your learning data**
- The words you've collected, along with any translation you've written yourself, personal notes,
  and the sentence you pasted when you added the word (this can be anything — a line from a book,
  a text message, whatever you were reading when you met the word).
- Your review history: what you were asked, how you answered, how long you took, and the spaced-
  repetition scheduling data that comes from it.
- Your streaks and drill session history.

**Timezone**
Your device's timezone (detected automatically, or set manually in your profile) is stored so we
can work out when your day starts and ends — this is what your streak and your daily story are
based on.

**Generated content**
Stories and story cover images that Wortgarten generates for you, built from the words you've
collected. See "AI processing" below for how these are made.

**Technical data**
A session cookie that keeps you logged in. It's first-party (set by Wortgarten, not a third
party) and essential — the app doesn't work without it. We do not use any analytics or advertising
cookies, and we do not run any analytics or error-tracking service at all.

## Why we collect it

- **Account info** — to identify you, let you log in, and let you recover your account.
- **Timezone** — to work out when your day starts, for streaks and your daily story.
- **Words, notes, and source sentences** — this is the core of the app: it's what gets scheduled
  for review and what your stories are built from.
- **Review history** — to schedule what you review next (spaced repetition) and to show you your
  own progress.
- **Session records (IP, user agent)** — security and keeping you logged in.

## AI processing

Wortgarten generates a short story for you most days, built only from words you've already
collected. To write it, we send **the list of words you've collected** (not your notes, not your
source sentences, not your email) to an AI provider:

- **Groq**, a third-party AI API, is the primary option. Your word list is sent to Groq to write
  the story text.
- **Ollama**, running locally on our own server, is the fallback/offline option. When Ollama is
  used, your word list never leaves the server at all — it's processed entirely on hardware we
  control, not sent to any outside company.
- The story's cover image is generated by sending a short English description of the story (not
  your word list, not your notes) to **Google Gemini** (or, if configured, **Cloudflare's image
  model** — only one is active at a time), and the resulting image is stored in **Cloudflare R2**.

Wortgarten's own code always decides which words a story uses — the AI model never chooses your
vocabulary, it only writes around the list it's given.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers. The only places any data goes:

| Who | What they get | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Groq | Your collected-words list | Writes your daily story |
| Ollama (our own server) | Your collected-words list | Same as above — stays on our server, not sent anywhere |
| Google Gemini | A short English summary of a generated story | Draws the story's cover image |
| Cloudflare (R2, and optionally Workers AI for images) | Generated cover images | Storage / alternate image generation |
| Google | Your name, email, and profile photo | Only if you choose "Sign in with Google" |

## Your rights

- **Access & export** — you can download all your collected words at any time from
  [your profile](/profile) ("Export my words," CSV or JSON).
- **Correction** — you can edit your display name from [your profile](/profile) at any time.
- **Deletion** — you can permanently delete your account from [your profile](/profile) ("Delete
  account"). This removes your account, your words, your review history, your stories, and their
  cover images.
- **Objection** — if you'd rather not have stories generated for you, or have any other concern
  about how your data is used, contact us (below) and we'll work it out with you directly.

## Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete
your account record, your collected words, your review and session history, your stories, and
their stored cover images. A small number of records tied to your email (like a pending password
reset) are cleaned up at the same time.

## Cookies

Wortgarten uses one cookie: a first-party session cookie that keeps you logged in. It is essential
— there is no way to use the app without it. We don't use tracking, advertising, or third-party
cookies, and we don't run any analytics.
